# Competitive positioning

## Boundary

Notion and Trello are strong general-purpose task and document tools. They can store a research task or a link, but they do not make the evidence hand-off itself explicit by default.

Evidence Sprint Board is deliberately narrower: each hand-off keeps the claim, owner, stage, source, publisher, access date, measurement unit, verification state and caveat together, then produces a brief that carries those fields forward.

## Honest distinction

- Notion/Trello answer: “What task is next, and who owns it?”
- Evidence Sprint Board adds: “What exactly can we claim, where did it come from, what is the unit and date, and what can it not establish?”
- It is not a replacement for a full research database, citation manager or synchronized team workspace.
- The differentiator is the evidence contract at the moment work changes hands, not the four-column board itself.

## Demo phrasing

“A task board can tell us that a source needs review. Evidence Sprint Board makes the review deliverable explicit: source, unit, date, owner and caveat travel with the claim into the one-page brief.”
